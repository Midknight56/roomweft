# User validation plan

Status on 10 Nov 2026: no sessions have been run yet. Until they are, RoomWeft makes no claim that it saves time,
money or returns for anyone. This page is the script we will use, so the result can be checked.

## Who

Three people who rent a small room and have bought furniture online in the last two years. Adults, unpaid, recruited
by asking friends and family. No names are kept.

## Consent (read aloud before starting)

"This is a short test of a furniture planning web page. You can stop at any time. I will write down what you do, how
long it takes and what you say. I will not record your name, face or voice. Your words may be quoted without your
name. Is that OK?"

## Tasks (no coaching; the moderator only reads the task)

| # | Task | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Your desk stays where it is. Find out if anything in this room is a problem." | They say the reading chair blocks the door |
| 2 | "Fix the problem." | The room shows all checks passing |
| 3 | "Is there a piece whose size the shop did not give?" | They name the unverified piece |
| 4 | "Pay for the basket with the test card." | The receipt shows |
| 5 | "Return one piece." | The refund shows completed |

Baseline: before RoomWeft, the same person gets the four product pages of the first design and the room size on paper,
and is asked task 1 only ("Would any of these block the door of this room?").

## What we record per person

Task done (yes, no, with help), seconds per task, wrong turns, and their exact words when they were unsure.
